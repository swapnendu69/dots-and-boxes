# Dots and Boxes

Dots and Boxes is a classic turn-based pencil-and-paper strategy game brought to life as a clean, offline-ready digital board game. Players take turns connecting adjacent dots with lines; completing the fourth side of a 1×1 square claims the box, awards a point, and grants an immediate bonus turn. The player with the most claimed boxes when the entire grid is completed wins the match.

---

## 📖 How to Play

1. **Select Game Mode & Settings**:
   - Choose between **vs CPU** (Single Player) or **Pass & Play** (Local Multiplayer).
   - Configure grid dimensions (from 3×3 up to 10×10).
   - For multiplayer, choose the number of players (2 to 10) and customize their names and colors.

2. **Take Turns**:
   - Players take turns drawing a horizontal or vertical line between two unlinked, adjacent dots.

3. **Complete Boxes & Bonus Turns**:
   - When a player draws a line that completes the fourth border of a square box, that box is claimed by that player, marked with their initial and color, and scores 1 point.
   - **Crucial Rule**: Completing a box awards an immediate extra turn! Players can chain multiple box completions in a single turn.

4. **Winning the Game**:
   - The game ends when every possible line has been drawn and all boxes are claimed.
   - The player with the highest total score wins. If multiple players tie for the top score, the game concludes in a draw.

## ✨ Features

- **Single Player vs CPU**: Play against an intelligent computer opponent powered by smart heuristics and tactical board evaluation.
- **Pass & Play Multiplayer**: Play locally on a single device with 2 to 10 friends and family.
- **Customizable Players**: Add personalized names and assign distinct signature colors to each player.
- **Variable Grid Sizes**: Choose board dimensions from quick 3×3 rounds to epic, strategic 10×10 showdowns.
- **100% Offline Gameplay**: Runs entirely on your device with no internet connection, no servers, and no data usage required.
- **No Ads & No In-App Purchases**: Pure, distraction-free board game experience with zero paywalls.
- **Interactive Visuals & Sound**: Responsive touch-friendly board, dynamic line drawing animations, and synthesized audio sound effects.
